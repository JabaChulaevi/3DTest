03 — The Observatory

A bureau with no territory.

The Bureau of Imaginary Meteorology was founded in 1961 by a cartographer named Ilse Vandermeer, who mistook a cloud for a continent and spent the following four years forecasting its weather. She was never corrected.

The Bureau has operated continuously since, issuing a bulletin every morning at 06:00 UTC to a subscriber list that has never been disclosed. We do not observe. We do not predict. We describe — with as much precision as the subject allows — weather that is happening somewhere it cannot happen.

All measurements are exact. None can be checked.
